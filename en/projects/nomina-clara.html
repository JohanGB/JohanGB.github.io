01Problem

  • Laws 2466 of 2025 and 2101 of 2021 change the rules on staggered dates: night work starts at 7 p.m. from 25 December 2025; the Sunday surcharge rises from 75 % to 80 %, 90 % and 100 % between 2025 and 2027; the maximum week drops from 47 to 42 hours.
  • The same shift is paid differently depending on its date, so the engine evaluates every minute with the rule in force that day.

02Data

  • 36 rules in reglas/parametros.yaml with value, validity window, source, status, norm, article, official URL, consultation date and literal quote. No legal value is hard-coded in Python.
  • Colombian holidays and the shift roster to compute.

03Method

  • Surcharges and overtime minute by minute, split by component (night, overtime, Sunday), with daily and weekly cap alerts.
  • Occasional and habitual compensatory rest; part-time work with proportional minimum wage (Decree 2616 of 2013).
  • Employer cost with contributions, the art. 114-1 exemption and the IBC cap.
  • Settlement for indefinite, fixed-term and per-project contracts: severance, interest, bonus, vacation, art. 64 indemnity and art. 65 late-payment penalty.

04Validation

  • "Verified" means the value and validity were read from a primary source on the recorded date; the loader rejects a verified rule without URL and date.
  • Every result line carries its citations.
  • 72 automated tests, including property-based tests with hypothesis.

05Limitations

  • Not every rule is verified yet: those that are not stay marked "to verify" with the exact link to their source.
  • Version 0.3: employer view only for now; the employee view comes next.
  • Not legal advice. Every citation links to the official text; check it before a real case.

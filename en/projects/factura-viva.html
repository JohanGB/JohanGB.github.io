01Problem

It turns DIAN electronic invoices (the ZIP files that arrive by email) and bank statements into concrete answers: where the money went, how much really left the accounts, how much can be deducted for the 1 % of e-invoiced purchases, and which products went up in price.

02Data

  • Invoices: ZIP → AttachedDocument XML → UBL 2.1 invoice, de-duplicated by CUFE.
  • Statements: templates for AV Villas (credit card), Caja Social, Nequi, Nu account and Nu card, plus a generic reader for new banks.
  • Personal data never leaves the local folder; account numbers are kept as last four digits only. PDF passwords are requested at the prompt and never stored.

03Method

  • Rule-based categories in YAML, recording which rule decided.
  • Real outflow: statement outflows minus transfers between my own products.
  • Deduction: 1 % of e-invoiced purchases, capped at 240 UVT (art. 336-5, Tax Code).
  • Price alerts: same product and issuer, current price vs. previous median.

04Validation

  • 9 checks per invoice: lines plus taxes equal total, CUFE and DIAN validation.
  • Opening balance plus movements must equal closing balance; otherwise it goes to a review queue.
  • Immutable raw data: if a raw file changes, the pipeline stops. Each row keeps source file, SHA-256, line or row and code version.
  • 95 automated tests; in CI, 93 pass and 2 are skipped.

05Limitations

  • Version 0.2, phase 1: a useful MVP without machine learning. It still needs 20 to 50 labelled invoices to measure per-category F1 and train a model against the rule baseline.
  • Price alerts need two purchases of the same product on different dates.
  • Not tax or financial advice.
